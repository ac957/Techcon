Techcon is an annual industrial conference aimed for programmers, engineers or individuals aspiring to seek jobs in these fields to come together and talk about what they love. Everyone is welcome to attend techcon old and young! Techcon provides opportunities for individuals to network, make friends with the same interests, learn new skills and have early access to the latest technology. Big names in the tech community will also be coming in on each day of techcon to speak about there chosen topics and what inspired them to persue a career in the tech industry. More infromation about the speakers can be found on the speakers page.
